About Us

Get to know us — a local company built for a global market.

◆ Incorporated 6 May 2011

2En Apps Sdn. Bhd. is a local company with a global presence in technology services, management consulting and outsourcing — founded by a group of experienced personnel from various industries, specialising in ICT services and product delivery.

2En Apps was born from the idea of bringing a local company to the global market. Although our footprint abroad is still small, we believe that one day we will become one of the leading technology companies in the region.

We offer a comprehensive range of professional services, including custom-made system development across various platforms, system and hardware installation and configuration, and the supply of commercial hardware and software products for domestic and international clients.

We are equally committed to downstream activities such as consultancy, application system development and implementation — including System Design Consultancy and Project Management — and aspire to become a comprehensive, value-added network and service provider.

Our core value is to deliver great business value to our clients. Empowered by skilled staff and proven methodologies, we are technologically and financially sound in delivering the tasks entrusted to us.
